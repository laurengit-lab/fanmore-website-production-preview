About

About FanMore.

FanMore creates value for teams, leagues, venues, partners and fans everywhere they follow the game, inside the venue and far beyond it.

Company

Fans give their loyalty.
We give it value.

FanMore is a loyalty rewards platform built for professional sports and entertainment. It rewards fans for the loyalty they already show in the building, at home, at the bar or on the go, and turns that loyalty into lasting value for teams, leagues, venues and partners.

Most fans never set foot in the venue on game day. FanMore reaches them wherever they follow the game, addressing a significant untapped market opportunity. By applying the proven airline-style rewards model to sports and entertainment, FanMore is designed to deepen the connection with fans beyond traditional touchpoints, turning game-day passion into year-round engagement.

Built to integrate with existing team and league digital channels, FanMore brings together fan activation, first-party data insights and commercial partnerships within a single, scalable solution. This enables sports organizations to better understand their audiences and unlock new revenue streams, while giving brand partners more targeted and measurable ways to reach fans.

The Company’s vision is to make loyalty a more meaningful part of the fan experience and a sustainable driver of business growth across the industry.
